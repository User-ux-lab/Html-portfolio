About Me

Building Brands Through Strategic Digital Marketing

I'm a results-driven digital marketing specialist with over 6 years of experience in social media strategy, SEO optimization, and content creation. My passion is helping brands connect with their audiences through authentic, data-driven marketing campaigns.

Based in Toronto with a North American focus, I work with startups, small businesses, and established brands to develop comprehensive marketing strategies that drive growth, engagement, and measurable results. My expertise spans social media management, search engine optimization, content strategy, and digital advertising.

My Approach: I believe marketing should be strategic, authentic, and measurable. Rather than applying one-size-fits-all solutions, I take time to understand each client's unique business goals, audience, and competitive landscape to create tailored strategies that deliver real impact.

When I'm not managing campaigns or optimizing SEO, you'll find me exploring new marketing trends, testing growth strategies, or developing web applications to solve marketing problems.
